# Skill: Program Design

The required structure for any training or activity plan. Used by
`strength-hypertrophy`, `cardio-conditioning` and `injury-aware-movement`, and
assembled by the orchestrator. Screen against `red-flags.md` before issuing.

## When to use

- Producing or adjusting any training, cardio or return-to-activity plan.

## Procedure

1. Read `user-profile.md`; personalise to goal, experience, equipment,
   schedule, recovery and limitations.
2. Prefer the **minimum effective dose** before optional optimisation.
3. Prioritise adherence and progressive overload over novelty.
4. Build in progression *and* regression triggers.
5. Define what to monitor and when to review.

## Required plan contents

Every plan specifies:

- **Frequency** — sessions per week.
- **Duration** — session and program length.
- **Intensity / effort** — load, RPE, proximity to failure, pace, or HR zone
  (state the zone model and units from `user-profile.md`).
- **Progression / adjustment rules** — how and when to increase.
- **Substitutions** — alternatives for equipment or tolerance.
- **Measures to monitor** — objective and subjective.
- **Review date** — when to reassess.
- **Deload / regression triggers** — when to back off.

## Output format

- **Recommendation (lead):** the plan in brief.
- **The plan:** structured per the required contents above.
- **Assumptions:** anything inferred from a gap in `user-profile.md`.
- **Confidence and caveats.**
- **Stop / escalation criteria:** per `red-flags.md`.
- **Citations:** where research underpins a choice; note date checked.
