# Age Context — Men Approaching and In Their Forties

Preventive-health and training topics that become more relevant for men around
the late thirties and forties. This file lists **topics to surface and verify**,
not fixed numbers. Screening ages and clinical thresholds vary by country and by
personal and family risk, and they change over time.

> Educational only. Not a diagnosis or a screening schedule. Confirm anything
> here against **current national guidance** (see `user-profile.md` for which
> body applies, e.g. NHS or USPSTF) and with a clinician. Do not assert specific
> numeric thresholds from memory — look them up at the time of advice and cite
> the guideline version and date checked (see `evidence-standard.md`).

## How agents should use this file

- Treat these as **prompts to raise proactively**, not tasks to perform.
- When a topic here is relevant to the user's question, mention it, then defer
  the specifics to current guidance and a clinician.
- Never present a screening interval, target value or dose as settled without
  verifying it against an authoritative source at the time.

## Cardiovascular health

- Risk assessment tools become more meaningful in this age band (e.g. QRISK in
  the UK, ASCVD in the US) — verify current tool, inputs and thresholds.
- Blood pressure and lipids are common check-up topics; some countries offer a
  midlife health check (e.g. the NHS Health Check age range).
- The cardio agent covers *training*, not screening — surface screening as a
  separate, clinician-owned topic.

## Metabolic health

- Fasting glucose / HbA1c and central adiposity (waist circumference) are risk
  markers distinct from scale weight or BMI alone.
- The nutrition agent already separates weight from fat and lean mass; add
  central-adiposity risk as a topic to raise, not a number to assert.

## Body composition and training adaptation with age

- Resistance training and adequate protein support retention of lean mass into
  midlife — within existing agents' remit; frame it by age when relevant.
- Recovery capacity, tendon adaptation and joint tolerance may shift; progress
  load conservatively and weight warm-up and recovery accordingly.
- Protein distribution across the day is a reasonable topic; give ranges and
  verify current recommendations rather than asserting a fixed figure.

## Sleep

- Screen for sleep apnoea risk where symptoms suggest it (loud snoring, witnessed
  pauses, unrefreshing sleep, excessive daytime sleepiness) — escalate per
  `safety-boundaries.md` rather than managing in-app.

## Mental health

- Midlife men are an important group for mood, stress and mental-health topics.
- Surface supportively and proactively; apply the escalation rules for
  mental-health risk in `safety-boundaries.md`.

## General preventive topics worth raising (verify locally)

- Routine check-ups and any age-based screening offered in the user's country.
- Vaccinations relevant to age and health status.
- Alcohol, smoking/vaping and stress as modifiable risk factors.

## What this file must never do

- Bake in specific screening ages, target values, intervals or doses.
- Replace a clinician's assessment or a country's official schedule.
- Imply the user has, or does not have, any condition.
